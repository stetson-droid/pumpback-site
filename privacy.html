Privacy policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Pumpback is a service of Auto Motion Studios LLC, Lubbock, Texas. This policy explains how we handle information you submit through our website or a Pumpback lead form, including Facebook and Instagram instant forms.

What we collect

When you fill out a Pumpback lead form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your company website
  • Your answers to our form questions, such as whether you own or run a septic pumping company, whether you have at least two years of service records, and roughly how many pump-outs you run per month

If you email us, we also keep the content of that message and your email address.

How we use this information

We use this information only to contact you about Pumpback services and to book a call with you. We may also use it to confirm that Pumpback is a fit for your business, to follow up on a booked or missed call, and to keep a record of the conversation you asked us to have.

We do not use this information to advertise to you on other platforms, and we do not add you to a general marketing list without a separate request from you.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell your name, phone number, email address, company website, or form answers. We do not share this information with other companies for their own marketing.

We may share information with service providers who help us run our email, calendar, or forms, and only so they can provide that service to us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep lead and contact information only as long as we need it to respond to you, book and complete a call, or meet a legal record-keeping duty. If you ask us to delete it and we do not have to keep it by law, we will delete it.

How to request deletion or ask a question

To ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to request that we delete it, email us at stetson@automotionstudios.com. Tell us the name, phone number, or email you used on the form so we can find your record. We will handle the request as promptly as we reasonably can.

Who to contact

Pumpback, a service of Auto Motion Studios LLC, Lubbock, Texas.
Email: stetson@automotionstudios.com

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
